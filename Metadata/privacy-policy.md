# Privacy Policy — Home Sync Assistant

_Last updated: 7 September 2026_

Home Sync Assistant does not collect anything.

## What the app stores

- **Your Home Assistant servers** — the name and address you type for each one. These are kept
  on the device and shared with your other devices through your personal cloud account, so the
  app looks the same everywhere you use it.
- **Your access tokens** — kept in the keychain, marked to travel through your personal
  encrypted keychain sync. They are never written into the shared configuration record.
- **Your choices** — which pair you last synced, the port for the local API, and the activity
  log the app shows you. All of it stays on your devices.

## What the app sends, and where

- **To your Home Assistant server, and nowhere else.** The app opens a connection to the address
  you entered, using the token you provided, to read areas, entities and names, and to write the
  changes you apply. Nothing about you or your home is sent to the developer or to any third
  party.
- **To your own network, if you switch it on.** The optional local API answers requests from
  programs on your network. It serves only your own home data, and only on the device it runs on.

## What the app does not do

- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.
- No accounts, no sign-up, no email address.
- No servers operated by the developer. There is no backend.

## Purchases

The app is free. It offers a single optional tip, handled entirely by the platform's payment
system. The developer receives no payment details.

## Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects nothing, from anyone.
